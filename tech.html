Product, Tech & AI

I want to build products that are deeply loved and that earn the trust people place in them. That takes product sense and taste paired with a real understanding of both the user's problem and the technology underneath it. Understanding both is what lets you calibrate delight, predictability, and guardrails, so the product delivers on what it promised and fails gracefully when it can't, without spending the trust it took to get there. Here's what I'm building and writing about.
